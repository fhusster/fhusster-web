Privacy Policy

For the Fhusster studio website. Last updated August 18, 2026.

This policy covers the Fhusster studio website only. The games have their own privacy policies, linked from each game's page. For example, see the Heavy Bag privacy policy.

What I collect

Nothing, beyond what web hosting requires. This site has no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising scripts. I cannot see who visits or what they do here. The only information I receive is what you choose to send me through the contact form or by email.

Hosting logs

The site is served by the hosting provider (Vercel), which, like effectively all web hosts, may process standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and requested pages) to deliver the site and keep it secure. I do not use these logs to identify visitors.

Cookies and session storage

This site sets no cookies. It stores a single flag in your browser's session storage (fhusster_intro_seen) so the logo intro animation plays only once per visit. This flag never leaves your device, contains no personal information, and is discarded automatically when you close the browser.

Fonts

Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font file, Google receives the request in the same way it would for any web resource. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles such requests.

Contact form

Messages sent through the form on the Contact page are delivered to my inbox by FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service. The name, email address, and message you enter pass through that service solely so they can reach me. I use them only to reply to you.

Email

If you email me at fhussterdev@gmail.com, I use your message and address only to reply to you. I don't add you to mailing lists or share your address with anyone.

Changes

If these practices change, I will update this page and its date.

Contact

Questions? Write to fhussterdev@gmail.com. Fhusster is based in Ottawa, Canada.
