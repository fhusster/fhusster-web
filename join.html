Help me tune every hit before launch.

I believe games are better when they're shaped by the people who actually play them.

When one of my games enters beta, I invite people on my TestFlight list to play it before release and share their feedback. It's an opportunity to experience my games early, help me catch the little things I might have missed, and be part of the process as I refine them.

Your email will be used solely to add you to my TestFlight beta pool and provide access to games when they become available for testing. I won't use it for marketing, newsletters, or anything unrelated to beta access.

No commitment beyond playing the game and telling me what you think.
