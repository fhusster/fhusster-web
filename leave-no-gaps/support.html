Leave No Gaps Support

Answers first. If yours isn't here, the inbox is at the bottom.

Controls

Leave No Gaps plays in portrait, one hand is enough. The game picks where a piece will land; you pick how it is turned.

  • Swipe right or left to rotate the piece a quarter turn.
  • Swipe up to turn it half way round.
  • Tap to mirror it. The tap unlocks at level 11 and does nothing before that.
  • Swipe down to drop faster, up to four swipes.

The ghost under the piece shows exactly where that turn will land. A solid ghost fits. A ghost drawn with red crosses would leave a gap. The game never turns a piece for you.

Mistakes

A piece that lands with a gap does not join the tower. The top row under it bursts and its cubes settle into the lowest spots nearby. The first miss on a level costs only that row; each miss after it costs one more row, up to the level's cap. The line at the bottom of the screen tells you what the next miss would cost. Some daily challenges end on the first miss; the ready card says so before you start.

The camera

The camera rises on its own and never pauses, not for a miss, not for an animation. If the whole core of the tower drops below the bottom of the screen the run is over. On a level the camera stops at the finish line; after that there is no time pressure left and you fill the core to win.

Modes

Levels: 100 of them, each with its own foundation, colour and finish line. Clearing one unlocks the next. Endless unlocks with level 31: no finish line, a camera that speeds up on a clock, and your best height, level and time kept. Daily: one challenge a day, the same for everyone, rated easy, medium or hard; retries replay the same pieces. Daily clears do not unlock levels.

Progress

Progress is stored on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes it. The gear on the title screen holds the sound, haptics and announcer settings, your lifetime totals, and a reset that starts everything over.

Sound and the announcer

Sound effects, haptics, the announcer and his volume each have their own switch in the settings card. Reduce Motion in iOS Settings calms the tower's effects; the sounds stay.

Beta testers

Leave No Gaps is currently in TestFlight. Screenshots and a sentence or two on what happened are the most useful feedback. TestFlight's own Send Beta Feedback button attaches your device details automatically.

Contact

For bugs or anything else Leave No Gaps: contact@fhusster.com. Include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug. I reply within a few days.
