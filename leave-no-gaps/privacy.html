Leave No Gaps Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026. The short version: the game collects nothing.

Leave No Gaps is made by Fhusster, an independent studio in Ottawa, Canada. This policy covers the Leave No Gaps app for iPhone. The studio website has its own privacy policy.

What the game collects

Nothing. Leave No Gaps has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no servers of its own. It does not connect to the internet. Nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

What is stored on your device only

Your progress is kept in the app's own storage on your phone: which levels are unlocked and cleared, your best times and fewest mistakes, your endless record, your daily challenge results, lifetime totals, and your settings for sound, haptics and the announcer. Deleting the app deletes all of it. The reset in the settings card does the same without deleting the app.

Sharing

The result cards have a Share button that opens the iOS share sheet with a short line of text about your run. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to send it to, and what happens then is governed by that app's policy.

TestFlight

While the game is in beta it is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. TestFlight may collect crash logs and usage information and share them with the developer under Apple's privacy policy and the terms you accepted when you joined. Feedback you send through TestFlight comes with the device details you chose to include.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of the game starts doing something this policy does not cover, such as a leaderboard, the policy will be updated here first and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Leave No Gaps: contact@fhusster.com.
