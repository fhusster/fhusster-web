About Us

A small studio. A precise point of view.

Fhusster is a one-person independent game studio based in Ottawa, Canada. We make arcade games built around timing, precision, and the satisfaction of getting better. Small in scale, deliberate in every detail.

Self-funded and self-published, we can give the details the attention they deserve: how a control responds, how a challenge builds, and how a well-timed move feels. Every decision starts with the player.

Explore Dad Reflex and Heavy Bag. Two different games, one shared instinct: make every input matter.
