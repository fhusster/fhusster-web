Side rails

They're already running — pinned to the left and right edges of this window, 20px in. Each rail is a 2px line, a 2px gap, and a second 2px line: magenta outward on the left, blue outward on the right. Every 8 seconds a hard-edged light runs down the left one, then up the right one, four flat brightness bands with no blur. Watch the edges for a few seconds.

Markup, once, just inside <body>:

<div class="rails" aria-hidden="true">
  <div class="rail rail--l"><span class="rail-light"></span></div>
  <div class="rail rail--r"><span class="rail-light"></span></div>
</div>

Your .site wrapper needs position:relative;z-index:1 so content stays above them. No script.

Tuning

The tuner writes CSS variables on :root. Whatever you settle on, the final numbers are the four --rib-* and three --rail-* defaults in the stylesheet.

Markup

One ribbon, copied as-is. The two tail spans have to come before the body so they paint underneath it.

<span class="ribbon">
  <span class="ribbon-tail ribbon-tail--l"></span>
  <span class="ribbon-tail ribbon-tail--r"></span>
  <span class="ribbon-body">Our Games</span>
</span>
