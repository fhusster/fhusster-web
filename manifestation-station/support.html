Manifestation Station Support

Answers first. If yours isn't here, the inbox is at the bottom.

Getting started

Pick a manifestation from the Library or write your own, then build a routine on the Today tab: choose up to three modes (Say it, Type it, Hear it), how many times to repeat each one, and the days and time it runs. A routine counts toward your streak once every step is done.

Say it

Say mode needs microphone and speech recognition permission, and listens on your iPhone only. Your voice is never recorded or uploaded. A rep counts when the whole sentence is heard. If it keeps missing you, speak a little slower and closer to the phone. After 3 missed tries in a row on the same rep, a Tap to confirm button appears so you are never stuck. The first time you use a language, iOS may need a minute to download its speech model.

Type it

Type mode asks for the whole sentence, typed letter by letter. Autocorrect, suggestions, pasting and dictation are turned off on purpose so every rep is really typed.

Hear it and recording your voice

Every ready-made manifestation comes with a natural voice in English and in French. For a sentence you wrote yourself, tap Record in your voice: record it once, check it, and trim it if you like. A sentence of your own has no voice until you record it. Hear plays with the screen locked and through headphones, at 1x up to 2x, and the pause between reps can be changed in Settings.

Frequencies

Ten frequencies are in Library, under Frequencies. Each one downloads from Apple the first time you tap it (about 25 MB) and then plays offline. It keeps playing through your routines. If a download fails, check your connection and tap the track again.

Reminders and daily manifestations

Each scheduled routine can remind you when it's time. In Settings, under Notifications, you can also receive 1 to 12 manifestations through the day, in your own voice if you recorded one. If nothing arrives, check that notifications for Manifestation Station are allowed in iOS Settings and that Focus isn't silencing them.

Streaks and grace days

Your streak counts the scheduled days you finished your routines. Days with nothing scheduled don't break it. Every 7 days in a row earns a grace day (up to 2 saved), which covers a missed day automatically. A routine counts for the day it started, so a night routine that runs past midnight still counts for that evening.

Help me write

Help me write uses Claude, Anthropic's AI, to suggest manifestations from a goal you describe or to improve one you wrote. It is optional, asks for your consent first, and sends only the text you type. It is available in the regions Anthropic supports, needs an internet connection, and allows 10 requests a day and 50 a month. You can turn it off in Settings. Suggestions come from an AI and may not always be right for you; you can edit them before saving.

Language

The app is in English and French and follows your iPhone's language. To change it for this app only, open iOS Settings, then Apps, then Manifestation Station, then Language. Each manifestation also has its own language, which is the one Say mode listens for.

Your data

There is no account. Your manifestations, routines, streaks and recordings stay on your iPhone. In Settings you can export your data as a file or delete all of it. Deleting the app also deletes everything. See the privacy policy for details.

Not medical advice

Manifestation Station supports habit building and reflection. It is not medical advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a local help line; in Canada and the US you can call or text 988.

Contact

For bugs, questions, or anything else Manifestation Station: contact@fhusster.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug. I reply within a few days, in English or French.
