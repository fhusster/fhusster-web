Three Ways to Practice

01 / Say it

Speak it out loud.

Speak your manifestation out loud. The app listens and counts each correct repetition, right on your device.

02 / Type it

Write it by hand.

Type the full sentence by hand, no autocorrect, no suggestions, no shortcuts.

03 / Hear it

Play it on a loop.

Record it in your own voice and play it back on a loop, even with the screen locked.

More Features

  • Mix all three modes in one routine, morning, night or any time
  • Ready-made manifestations, or write your own
  • Daily reminders throughout the day
  • Calendar and streaks to track your consistency
  • English and French

Writing Help, Powered by Claude

Not sure how to word it? Describe your goal in plain words and Claude, Anthropic's AI, helps you write and refine manifestations, build a routine that fits your day, and translate between English and French. AI features are optional and clearly labelled.

Privacy

No account needed. Your voice recordings and speech recognition stay on your device. Text is only sent to the AI when you choose to use a writing feature.

Pricing

One-time purchase. No subscription. No ads.

Manifestation Station supports habit building and reflection. It is not medical advice.
