Three Ways to Practise

01 / Say it

Speak it out loud.

Say your manifestation and the app listens, on your iPhone. When the whole sentence is heard, the rep counts and the sentence lights up. Your voice is never recorded or uploaded.

02 / Type it

Write it by hand.

Type the full sentence, letter by letter. No autocorrect, no suggestions, no paste, no dictation. Every rep is really typed.

03 / Hear it

Play it on a loop.

Record it in your own voice and let it play on a loop, even with the screen locked. No recording yet? A natural voice reads it until you make your own.

Built Around Your Routine

  • Routines that stack the modes, for example hear it 3 times, say it 6 times, then type it 9 times
  • Schedule each routine for the mornings, nights or days that suit you, with a reminder when it's time
  • 175 ready-made manifestations in seven categories, written natively in English and in French, or write your own
  • Manifestations delivered through the day as notifications, in your own voice if you like
  • A calendar and streaks that track your consistency, with grace days for the day life gets in the way
  • Fully in English and French, following your iPhone's language
  • Export your data any time, or delete it all in one tap

Writing Help, Powered by Claude

Not sure how to word it? Describe your goal in plain words and Claude, Anthropic's AI, suggests manifestations you can save or edit. Already wrote your own? Claude can help tighten it. AI features are optional, clearly labelled, and available in supported regions.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your manifestations, routines, streaks and voice recordings stay on your iPhone, and speech recognition runs on the device. Help me write only sends the text you type, only after you agree to it, and only when you tap the button. Fhusster does not store that text. Read the Manifestation Station privacy policy.

Pricing

One-time purchase. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases.
