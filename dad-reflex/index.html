Pillow-catching arcade game

Dad Reflex

Babies are falling. You have a pillow. Pull the meter, time the throw, and slide a cushion under a tumbling toddler before the floor gets there first. Miss, and you have a mess to mop up. Let enough mess pile up at once and the level is over. Built for iOS.

Download on the App Store Out Now

The Game

One hundred hand-built levels, each in its own nursery. Pull the power meter to launch a pillow across the floor: a light throw crawls, a red-zone throw sprints, and the whole game is knowing which one this baby needs. Catch one on the cushion and it rides off the screen safe. Drop one and it leaves a mess, which you clear by tapping it or sweeping it away under the next pillow. Let too many pile up at once and the level ends. The floor gets less forgiving as you climb: ten messes early on, three from level 71.

Thirty-eight pillow designs wait in the Boutique, four of them reserved for dads who clear 25, 50, 75 and all 100 levels without dropping a single baby. Hearts are revives: spend one to wipe the floor and keep the run alive. Away from the campaign there is Survival, a single run that outpaces level 100 at around the five-minute mark and only ends when the floor does, with a Game Center leaderboard for the longest night. And there is Online, a ranked two-minute duel where both dads get the same babies in the same order and the higher score takes the points. One hundred achievements across all three.

Get It

Dad Reflex is out now on the App Store for iPhone. One download, no account, no sign-up. Game Center handles the leaderboard and the achievements if you want them, and the game plays perfectly well if you don't.
