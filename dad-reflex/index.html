Pillow-catching arcade game

Dad Reflex

Babies are falling. You have a pillow. Get it under them before the floor does. Miss one and you are mopping the nursery while three more come down. Built for iPhone.

Download on the App Store Out Now

The Game

Babies are falling. You have a pillow. That's the plan. Pull back, estimate how much power you need, and release at just the right moment. Pull too little and the pillow falls short. Pull too far and it flies past the baby. There's no aiming, just timing, instinct, and learning exactly how much pull you need. Get it right and congratulations, a baby is saved! Miss the save and the baby lands on the ground, but thankfully, their poop-filled diaper provides a little cushion. The baby bounces back up laughing, while you're left with a pile of poop to clean. Keep the floor clean, keep the babies getting swept to safety, and whatever you do, don't let the nursery fill up.

There are 100 levels, with the difficulty steadily increasing as you go. What starts as a simple "pull and release" quickly becomes a test of timing, power, and, apparently, your dad reflex. Earn coins as you play and spend them on new pillows, while other pillows can only be unlocked by completing achievements.

Think you've got what it takes to make the leaderboard? Survival Mode and Online Mode each have their own leaderboard, giving you two different ways to compete. Survival keeps the babies coming faster and faster until you finally can't keep up. Online puts you face-to-face with another dad in a 2-minute round, with the same babies, in the same order. One run tests how long you can survive. The other tests who can save more. Two modes. Two leaderboards. One question: how good are your dad reflexes?
