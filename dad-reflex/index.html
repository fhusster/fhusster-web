Pillow-catching arcade game

Dad Reflex

Babies are falling. You have a pillow. Get it under them before the floor does. Miss one and you are mopping the nursery while three more come down. Built for iPhone.

Download on the App Store Out Now

The Game

Drag the meter, let go, and the pillow slides. How far you pull is the whole game — too soft and it stops short, too hard and it shoots straight past the kid you were aiming at. Land it and the baby bounces off happy. Miss and there is a mess on the floor, and you are now doing two jobs at once: tapping the mess away with one thumb while the next baby is already falling. Let the floor fill up and you are done. One hundred levels, each in its own nursery, and they stop being generous surprisingly early.

Every pillow you throw can be a different one. Some you buy, some you have to earn, and a few of them only turn up for dads who can clear a level without dropping anybody. There are one hundred achievements to chase, and at least one pillow nobody gets to see until they have chased all of them.

When the campaign stops being enough: Survival is one run that keeps speeding up until it is harder than level 100 and only ends when the floor does — there is a leaderboard for the longest night. Online puts you against another dad for two minutes flat, same babies, same order, higher score wins.

Get It

Dad Reflex is out now on the App Store for iPhone. One download, no account, no sign-up. Game Center handles the leaderboard and the achievements if you want them, and the game plays perfectly well if you don't.
