Kickboxing / Muay Thai rhythm game

Heavy Bag

Strike to the beat. Heavy Bag turns kickboxing and Muay Thai training into a gesture-driven rhythm game. Punches, elbows, knees, and kicks land with a swipe, all in crunchy 16-bit pixel art. Built for iOS.

Join the TestFlight Beta In Beta

The Game

Every track is a training session. Strikes scroll in on the beat, and your job is to land the right technique at the right moment: jab, cross, elbow, teep, roundhouse. The gesture controls are tuned until they feel like second nature. Miss the timing and the bag lets you know.

No stamina meters, no loot, no filler. Just you, the rhythm, and a heavy bag that keeps score.
