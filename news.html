November 17, 2026

Leave No Gaps enters beta

A second game is in TestFlight. Leave No Gaps is a timing-based tower builder for iPhone: pieces fall toward a tower five columns wide, you turn them to fit, and a camera climbs the screen without ever waiting for you. Fill the tower to the line without a gap and the whole thing bursts at once. On the way up every clean fit pops, a streak lights bulbs up both sides, and an announcer cheers every fifth in a row, more impressed each time, all the way to a hundred.

There are 100 levels, an Endless run against a camera on a clock, and a daily challenge drawn from 97 twists on the rules. No account, no ads, nothing collected. If you want to help me tune it, request beta access.

November 17, 2026

Manifestation Station is out now on the App Store

Fhusster's first app is live. Manifestation Station turns affirmations into a daily routine you actually do: say them out loud, type them by hand, or hear them on a loop, and the app checks every repetition. It started as my wife's idea, and now we both use it every day.

200 ready-made manifestations with natural recorded voices, in English and in French, or write your own and record it in your voice. Claude can help you word one if you get stuck. One-time purchase, no subscription, no account. Download Manifestation Station on the App Store, and if something looks wrong, the support page is the place to tell me.

October 23, 2026

Dad Reflex is out now on the App Store

Fhusster's first release is live. Dad Reflex, the pillow-catching arcade game, is on the App Store for iPhone today: one hundred levels, an endless Survival run, ranked two-minute online duels, and 38 pillows to collect. One download, no account, no sign-up.

Download Dad Reflex on the App Store. The Survival and Online leaderboards and all 100 achievements run through Game Center if you want them, and the game plays in full if you don't. If something looks wrong, the support page is the place to tell me.
